# Reader test — protocol (first run: 2026-09)

**Who.** One person preparing for a coding interview loop, comfortable with LeetCode Mediums,
not the author. Not a friend doing you a favour with feedback; someone who actually wants the practice.

**Send.** The link only, plus one sentence: "The AI models have already tried these ten problems;
your turn." Nothing else. No explanation of the ladder, the picker, or the reveal. If they need it
explained, that is a finding.

**Success criterion (from CONTEXT.md).** A second recorded attempt on a later day. Ask them to
paste the summary line from the bottom of the page after each session ("N of 10 rungs attempted,
M passed. Median time …"); the page keeps verdicts only in their browser, so that line is the only
record you will see.

**Do not ask during the first session.** Let it run.

**Ask after the first session, in this order, and write the answers down verbatim:**
1. Which rung did you start on, and why that one?
2. Did the model record change what you tried, or how long you kept going?
3. After you recorded a verdict, what did you read first: the idea, the pitfalls, or the solution?
4. Did you change the language in the picker? Was the solution readable without the manual?
5. Would the pitfalls have been more useful *before* the attempt? (The Gemini Pro review said yes;
   the design says no. This is the open question.)
6. What did you do when the card said "Next: rung N"?

**Ask after the second session, or after a week without one:**
7. What brought you back, or what would have?

**Record** the answers in `review/reader-1.md`, with the two summary lines. Then decide, in
CONTEXT.md, one of three: keep the reveal gated as designed; move the pitfalls before the attempt;
or stop. No feature work until that line exists.
