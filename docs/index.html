Hard Half · pilot · 10 problems

The models have already tried these. Your turn.

Ten problems from the hard half of recent LeetCode weekly contests, questions 3 and 4 only, and on every one of them at least one AI model failed. Each model's solutions were submitted to LeetCode in up to seven languages; the verdicts are under each problem. Start the clock, solve it wherever you solve things, record what happened, and the card opens: the idea that works, the mistakes the models made, and the full manual.

Rungs run from the problem most model submissions passed to the one most failed. Start wherever the failures interest you. Your times and verdicts stay in this browser only. A count like deepseek 5/7 means five of seven submissions accepted, one submission per language, so a family can pass in Python and fail in Java.

    Source of the model record: an independent benchmark that submitted model-written solutions to LeetCode and kept the verdicts. Counts are submissions, one per model and language, not distinct models. Manuals are generated from accepted solutions and are the reference, not the answer key. Problem statements are LeetCode's; links go to the original problems.
