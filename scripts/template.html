The models have already tried these. Your turn.

Ten problems from the hard half of recent LeetCode weekly contests, questions 3 and 4 only. Every one was submitted to LeetCode by a dozen AI models in up to seven languages, and the record of who got Accepted is below each problem. Start the clock, solve it however you solve things, then record what happened. Only then do you get the models' mistakes and the full manual.

How this works.
  1. Pick the lowest rung you have not cleared. The ladder is ordered by how many model attempts were accepted, so the first win comes early and the last rung is the one most models failed.
  2. Press Start, read, think, code wherever you like. Submit on LeetCode if you want a real verdict.
  3. Press Passed, Failed or Gave up. Your time and verdict stay in this browser only.
  4. The card then opens: what the models got wrong on this problem, and the link to the manual built from their accepted solutions.

    Source of the model record: an independent benchmark that submitted model-written solutions to LeetCode and kept the verdicts. Counts are submissions, one per model and language, not distinct models. Manuals are generated from accepted solutions and are the reference, not the answer key. Problem statements are LeetCode's; links go to the original problems.
